## Q3. Belarus law on collecting money from many individuals for one person

- The Law of the Republic of Belarus «О платежных системах и платежных услугах» (H12200164, i.e. Law No. 164-З of 2022) excludes from its scope: "services of an individual collecting money from other individuals and passing it to retail (service) organizations, where such services do not imply systematic profit for all parties", contributions to non-profits, and non-business collection services on a list set by the National Bank. | https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=H12200164 | pravo.by (official) | 2022 | accessed 2026-10-11 (search snippet; page refused connection) | medium | fact-primary
- Payment service providers, **including payment aggregators**, may operate only after inclusion in the NBRB register of payment service providers. Aggregators keep a guarantee fund set as a % of average monthly turnover of accepted payments. | https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=H12200164 ; https://pravo.by/novosti/novosti-pravo-by/2023/january/72714/ | pravo.by | 2022 / 2023-01 | accessed 2026-10-11 (snippet) | medium | fact-primary
- NBRB Instruction on providing payment services (B22239262) and a Jan 2023 regulation on non-financial payment service providers exist. Their contents were not read. | https://pravo.by/document/?guid=3871&p0=B22239262 ; https://pravo.by/novosti/novosti-pravo-by/2023/january/72714/ | pravo.by | 2022 / 2023 | accessed 2026-10-11 | low (contents unread) | fact-primary
- MNS (tax ministry) clarification: money one individual receives from others for shared expenses (holidays, joint trips, parents' committee) used to pay third parties for goods or services is not subject to the "offshore/social" levy on individuals. | https://news.zerkalo.io/economics/131650.html | Zerkalo | ~2019 | accessed 2026-10-11 (snippet) | low-medium | press
- A Belarusian decree limits individuals to one charitable account per beneficiary for medical fundraising, with consolidation required by 12 Nov 2026. Relevant only as a sign of tight regulation of collections. | pravo.by search snippet (exact URL not resolved) | pravo.by | 2026 | accessed 2026-10-11 | low | fact-primary
- bePaid is officially a payment aggregator in ERIP. No bePaid "collection/piggy bank" product was found. | https://devby.io/news/pervyy-poshel-s-dnem-rozhdeniya-bepaid | dev.by | n/d | accessed 2026-10-11 (snippet) | low | press
- Ulej.by is run by ООО «Хайв проджект». The same team launched MolaMola, a fast-fundraising platform (campaign in ~15 min, contributions gratuitous). Platform fees were not found. | https://devby.io/news/v-belarusi-platformu-bystrogo-sbora-sredstv-dlya-lyubykh-idei ; https://myfin.by/article/biznes/10296-kraudfanding-v-belarusi-kak-i-blagodarya-chemu-eto-rabotaet | dev.by / Myfin | n/d | accessed 2026-10-11 (snippet) | low | press
- Inference: a BY platform that takes money from several people and passes it to another *individual* (not a shop) does not fit the snippet-quoted exclusion, which covers transfers to retail organizations without profit. If it charges fees, it would likely need registration as a payment service provider or aggregator, or would have to route payments through a registered one. Safer pattern: pay the merchant directly (e.g. buy the item or a certificate). Needs a lawyer's confirmation. | (from the claims above) | — | — | — | low-medium | inference

## Q4. ЮKassa payouts / Safe Deal; WB gift collection

- ЮKassa Payouts: card payouts cost **from 2.8% + 30 ₽** per transaction. Limits are 5–500,000 ₽ per payout and up to 1.5M ₽ per card per month. Onboarding requires a questionnaire, a passport scan and an application. The page does not state eligibility by legal form or support for foreign cards. | https://yookassa.ru/payouts/ | ЮKassa (official) | n/d (live page) | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | fact-primary
- ЮKassa Safe Deal is for platforms where individuals sell to each other. It can run "without identifying users". Payouts go to bank cards and ЮMoney wallets, with limits on card types. ЮKassa takes its commission plus VAT from the platform's reward. No rates or foreign-card terms are on the page. | https://yookassa.ru/developers/solutions-for-platforms/safe-deal/basics | ЮKassa developers (official) | n/d | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | fact-primary
- WB launched a joint "chip in for a gift" collection in its app (TASS, Moscow). It can start from the cart or the "WB Банк" section. Participants contribute from any bank card or WB Кошелёк. If the target is not reached, the organizer can use the funds for any other WB item. It launched in test mode, to roll out to all users within weeks. Fees and country availability are not stated. | https://finance.mail.ru/article/na-wildberries-poyavilas-funkciya-dlya-sovmestnogo-sbora-deneg-na-podarok-69024753/ | Mail.ru Finance / TASS | 2025-12-09 | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | press
