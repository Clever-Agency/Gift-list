Some partners are paid in "points" instead of roubles. | https://yandex.ru/support/market-distr/ru/terms | Yandex | undated | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | terms

### RF ad-labeling law and the 3% levy
- Since 1 Sept 2022, each internet ad creative needs an ERID token, and its data goes to ЕРИР via an ОРД. The label must contain "Реклама", the advertiser (or site name) and the erid. Tokens are bound to the original contract chain. | https://yandex.ru/support/browser-b2c-distr/ru/labeling ; https://yandex.com/support/market-distr/ru/ad-labeling/ | Yandex | undated | accessed 2026-10-11 | med-high | legal
- The 3% levy on internet-ad revenue applies from 1 Apr 2025 under Federal Law 479-ФЗ of 26.12.2024. It is paid by whoever earns revenue from distributing ads: рекламораспространители (sites, platforms), ad-system operators and intermediaries. The base is quarterly ad revenue. РКН calculates it from ЕРИР data and posts it in the payer's cabinet. Payment is due by the 5th of the third month after the quarter. ИП and самозанятые can be liable. | https://ppc.world/news/v-rossii-vstupil-v-silu-zakon-o-3-m-sbore-za-reklamu-v-internete/ ; https://www.sostav.ru/publication/reklamnyj-sbor-3-kak-budet-rabotat-novyj-nalog-na-internet-reklamu-74084.html | ppc.world / Sostav | 2025 | accessed 2026-10-11 (snippets) | med | legal
- Inference (unverified by legal text): a wishlist site showing labeled affiliate links would be a рекламораспространитель. It would then (a) need erid labeling, which the Yandex Market API can supply, and (b) owe 3% on that affiliate revenue. Primary law text on consultant.ru was not fetched. | — | — | — | — | low | legal

### Belarus ad law
- Amendments to the Law "О рекламе" (No. 353-З of 06.01.2024) took effect 12 July 2024, implemented by Council of Ministers resolution No. 484 of 08.07.2024. Paid placements under contract require the distributor to be in the **реестр рекламораспространителей**. Content with links, addresses or phone numbers of a business can be treated as advertising. No erid/ОРД-style labeling regime or turnover levy was found for BY. | https://news.zerkalo.io/economics/72813.html (snippet) ; https://pravo.by/novosti/obshchestvenno-politicheskie-i-v-oblasti-prava/2023/november/76063/ | Zerkalo / pravo.by | 2023-2024 | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | legal
- The BY antimonopoly ministry found 21vek.by in breach of the ad law for sending ad messages without consent. Bonus or points-balance notices were deemed not to be advertising. | https://affi.io/m/21vek-by search snippet (source news unclear) | — | undated | accessed 2026-10-11 | low | legal

## D3 — Offline retail x app partnerships

- Babylist "showrooms" (Beverly Hills: 18,000 sq ft, opened 18 Aug 2023; SoHo NYC: 20,000 sq ft, 2026) are built to drive online registry creation, not in-store sales. Every product has a QR code to scan and add to the registry or buy online, and there are concierge registry appointments. Sources disagree on whether the NYC store sells on site. | https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/babylist-delivers-with-first-baby-gear-showroom-in-beverly-hills/136357/ ; https://www.modernretail.co/operations/babylists-newest-arrival-is-a-new-york-showroom/ | Retail TouchPoints / Modern Retail | 2023 / 2026-03-23 | accessed 2026-10-11 | med-high | example
