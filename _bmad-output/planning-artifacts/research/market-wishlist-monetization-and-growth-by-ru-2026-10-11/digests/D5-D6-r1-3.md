# Digest D5–D6 (round 1, worker 3): group-gift collections and payments/legal in BY vs RU

Run date: 2026-10-11. Only evidence retrieved in this run counts. Some figures come from search-engine summaries of pages I did not fetch in full; those are marked (snippet) and capped at med confidence. Several official pages could not be fetched: bepaid.by refused the connection, rustore.ru returned 429, and yookassa.ru/nerezidentam returned 404.

## D5: Group-gift money collections in RU/BY

- Wildberries launched (in test mode) a joint "скинуться на подарок" collection on 9 Dec 2025. The organizer starts a collection from the cart, sends friends a unique link, and each friend pays any amount by any bank card or WB Wallet. The organizer can close it early and spend the money on any other WB item. The article gives no fees and doesn't say where funds are held. Funds appear to stay inside the WB ecosystem (an evidence-backed inference). | https://finance.mail.ru/article/na-wildberries-poyavilas-funkciya-dlya-sovmestnogo-sbora-deneg-na-podarok-69024753/ | Mail.ru Финансы (citing TASS) | 2025-12-09 | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | terms
- Sber has a "Собрать деньги, разделить чек" feature in the «Диалоги» section of СберБанк Онлайн, used for things like colleague gifts and school collections. Only СберБанк Онлайн users can take part. | https://brobank.ru/sber-razdeli-cek | Brobank | undated (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | terms
- Т-Банк: Black card holders can create a link or QR code to receive transfers, and the source mentions birthday or school collections. A sender following the link chooses to pay via T-Bank or Sber. | https://habr.com/ru/post/978884 | Habr | undated (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | low | terms
- Т-Банк P2P limits change from 15 Sep 2026. Free monthly SBP transfers to others: 100,000 ₽ without a subscription, 300,000 ₽ with Pro, unlimited with Premium/Private. Above the limit the fee is 0.5%, max 1,500 ₽ per transfer, and some card-number transfers become paid. | https://www.vbr.ru/novosti/banki/2026/09/11/za-perevodi-iz-t-banka-nacnyt-brat-komissiu/ | VBR.ru | 2026-09-11 (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | pricing
- Sber "стягивание" fee (2023 data): pulling money from a Sber card through another bank's app costs 1.25%, min 50 ₽, max 200 ₽. Status in 2026 is unknown. | https://www.vbr.ru/novosti/banki/2023/06/30/sberbank-yvelicil-stoimost/ | VBR.ru | 2023-06-30 (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | low (stale) | pricing
- ЮKassa «Безопасная сделка» (Safe Deal) is built for C2C platforms. ЮKassa, not the platform, holds the buyer's money for 1 to 180 days. Payouts go to bank cards and ЮMoney wallets; the page doesn't mention SBP. Deals can run without identifying either party. Only the platform's reward reaches the shop balance, and ЮKassa deducts its commission plus VAT from that reward. The page doesn't name eligible legal forms or give any fee percentage. | https://yookassa.ru/developers/solutions-for-platforms/safe-deal/basics | ЮKassa (official docs) | undated | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | terms
- The 2021 Safe Deal launch coverage says no identification is needed and only card details are required. Money can be frozen for up to 3 months (the current docs say 180 days). | https://www.comnews.ru/digital-economy/content/215247/2021-06-30/2021-w26/yukassa-zapuskaet-bezopasnuyu-sdelku-dlya-s2s-platform | ComNews | 2021-06-30 | accessed 2026-10-11 | med (stale) | terms
- 161-ФЗ: a payment aggregator (п. 31 ст. 3) is a legal entity engaged by a money-transfer operator to take part in transfers in favour of legal entities, ИП and "other persons" under ч. 13 ст. 14.1, for payments made with electronic means of payment. I could not confirm from retrieved text that an aggregator may collect money in favour of ordinary individuals. That supports the expectation that a startup can't legally hold money for a list owner itself and has to use a licensed operator's platform product such as Safe Deal or payouts. | https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_328176/3d0cac60971a511280cbba229d9b6329c07731f7/ | КонсультантПлюс | undated (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | legal
- A payment agent under 103-ФЗ accepts an individual's money in favour of a seller for that seller's goods or services. A 161-ФЗ aggregator must identify users and handle disputes. | https://pay.yandex.ru/blog/articles/kto-takoy-platezhnyy-agent | Yandex Pay blog | undated (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | legal
- Belarus: a search found no Belarusian bank product for collecting money from several people for a gift. The closest match was a single-person «Копилка» savings service (a self-funded deposit top-up) at a BY bank (offer PDF on belapb.by). | https://belapb.by/upload/sprint.editor/dc5/ngxty0b27nokicgv34tz1k83qnbtmipq/20oferta_20na_20zaklyuchenie_20dogovora_20na_20predostavleniya_20servisa_20Kopilka_1.pdf | Belagroprombank (offer) | undated (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | low | terms
- Apple 3.2.1(vii): apps may let one user give another a monetary gift without IAP, provided (a) the gift is fully optional and (b) 100% of the funds go to the recipient. A gift tied to digital content or services must use IAP. 3.2.2(iv) bars in-app fundraising for charities except by approved nonprofits. This directly governs the "friends chip in, money to list owner" flow: the service can't take a cut of the gift inside the iOS app. | https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ | Apple | undated (current page) | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | store-policy
- Apple 3.1.3(e): physical goods and services consumed outside the app must be paid for outside IAP (for example Apple Pay or card entry). (The brief cited 3.1.5; on the current page 3.1.5 covers cryptocurrencies.) | https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ | Apple | undated | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | store-policy

## D6: Payments and legal, BY vs RU

### (a) ЮKassa
- Standard card rate is 2.8% plus 1% per receipt, or a reduced rate if you use «Чеки от ЮKassa». VAT is added on top of the commission. SBP is priced individually. The tariff page covers cards "issued in Russia and abroad by NSPK partners", which in practice means Мир. The reduced-rate offer runs 28.01.2026–01.12.2026 and is for Russian legal entities and ИП. The page links to a self-employed section without giving its terms. | https://yookassa.ru/fees/ | ЮKassa (official) | offer 2026-01-28 | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | pricing
- The tariff page lists no rates for Выплаты (payouts), Safe Deal or split payments. | https://yookassa.ru/fees/ | ЮKassa | 2026 | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | pricing
- ЮKassa takes commission only on successful payments, with no monthly fee. Third-party aggregators quote rates "from 0.4% + VAT" (not verified at the source). | https://toolfox.ru/services/s/yookassa | Toolfox | undated (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | low | pricing
- ЮMoney wallet transfers to other cards or wallets need an identification status that only Russian citizens can get. | https://a2is.ru/catalog/platyozhnye-sistemy/yandeks-dengi-yumoney | a2is.ru | undated (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | low | terms

### (b) Belarus acquiring
- Assist Belarus works through partner banks. At Belagroprombank the rates are from 1.5% for the bank's own cards, from 2.49% for other BY banks' cards and from 2.7% for foreign cards (higher if settled to another bank: 1.7%, 2.65% and 2.9%). Service is free and connection takes 4–10 working days. Priorbank connects in 1 day from 1.5%. A bank contract is required, which implies a BY legal entity or ИП. | https://myfin.by/bank/belagroprombank/ekvajring/internet | Myfin.by | undated (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | pricing
- Historical structure: acquiring banks charge about 2.3%, and the processor (bePaid) charged 0.5% + a fixed fee or 0.7%. These figures are from 2015 and only show the shape of BY pricing (bank fee + processor fee). | https://devby.io/news/tarify-na-internet-ekvayring-v-belarusi-kto-i-chto-zarabatyvaet | dev.by | 2015-07-14 | accessed 2026-10-11 | low (stale) | pricing
- bePaid supports recurring payments and subscriptions: Tilda lists bePaid among the providers for recurring payments, and Salebot documents a bepaid_subscription_status cancel API. | https://tilda.cc/ru/answers/a/recurrent/ ; https://docs.salebot.pro/integration/payments/bepaid | Tilda; Salebot | undated | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | terms
- ЕРИП: a business can connect through the «Кабинет производителя услуг» in 1–2 banking days, or through an aggregator such as bePaid, which is an ЕРИП information aggregator. In one example, 2 BYN of a 100 BYN payment went to ЕРИП fees. Money credited via ЕРИП can be returned only with the seller's consent. | https://myfin.by/article/biznes/kak-prinimat-platezi-onlajn-5-pricin-podklucitsa-k-erip ; https://ilex.by/ip-torguet-cherez-internet-magazin/ | Myfin.by; iLex | undated (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | pricing

### (c) Store billing
- Apple: since 1 April 2026, "payment processing is no longer available for purchases made on the App Store" in Russia. New purchases, IAP and subscription renewals are unavailable unless paid from an existing Apple Account balance. Subscriptions that can't be charged are cancelled. Reported to follow an order from Russia's Ministry of Digital Development. Belarus isn't mentioned. | https://www.mactrast.com/2026/04/apple-turns-off-app-store-and-services-payments-in-russia/ (cites https://support.apple.com/en-us/126891) | MacTrast / Apple | 2026-04-02 | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | store-policy
- Apple 3.1.1: unlocking features or subscriptions inside the app requires IAP. 3.1.3(b) Multiplatform Services: apps may give access to content or subscriptions bought on the developer's website, provided those items are also available as IAP in the app. 3.1.3 intro: outside the US storefront, apps can't encourage users inside the app to buy another way, but developers may communicate outside the app. 3.1.3(a) reader apps (magazines, books, music, video only) may request the External Link Account Entitlement. | https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ | Apple | undated | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | store-policy
- Implication: in Russia, IAP can't be offered at all after 1 Apr 2026, which conflicts with 3.1.3(b)'s "also available as IAP" condition. How App Review handles a web-only subscription for RU users is unresolved. A developer-forum thread asks exactly this and had no retrieved official answer. | https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/815050 | Apple Developer Forums | undated | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | store-policy
- Google Play: billing for users in Russia was paused in March 2022. From 2 Aug 2022 the Payments policy requirement to use Play Billing does not apply to apps accepting payments from users in Russia. Downloads and updates of paid apps were blocked in Russia and Belarus. From 26 Dec 2024, seller services are suspended indefinitely for developers with a Russian disbursement bank account. | https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11950272 ; https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15685001 | Google | 2022 / 2024 (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | high | store-policy
- RuStore commission is reported at 15% (NashStore 12%), with special terms for low-turnover companies. Monetization for self-employed developers ended: new applications stopped 12 Dec 2025, and BillingClient/Pay SDK stopped working for them on 1 Feb 2026, so an ИП or legal entity is needed. RuStore has separate pages for legal entities, non-residents and "payments without installing RuStore", but none could be fetched (HTTP 429). | https://profile.ru/news/scitech/rossijskie-rustore-i-nashstore-zamenili-zarubezhnye-magaziny-prilozhenij-na-os-android-1327870/ ; https://xn--80aapgyievp4gwb.xn--p1ai/blog/rustore-otmenyaet-monetizaciyu-samozanyatym | Profile.ru; blog | 2026-07-25; undated | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | store-policy

### (d) Taxes
- Belarus: foreign organisations providing electronic services to Belarusian individuals must register with ИМНС for Minsk and calculate and pay VAT themselves (Tax Code art. 141 and related). Registration is remote via the «е-НДС иностранной организации» account (RU/EN). Returns are quarterly by the 20th, with payment by the 22nd. The list of e-services follows EEC Council decision No. 97 of 27.09.2023, applied from 1 Apr 2024. | https://ilex.by/news/nalogooblozhenie-elektronnyh-uslug-zarubezhnyj-opyt-dlya-belarusi/ ; https://neg.by/novosti/otkrytj/nds-pri-okazanii-uslug-v-elektronnoy-forme-novye-podkhody-v-eaes/ | iLex; НЭГ | undated (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | tax
- The Belarusian VAT rate on foreign e-services to individuals is the standard 20%, in force since 2018. | https://neg.by/novosti/otkrytj/k-sboru-naloga-na-google-gotovy | НЭГ | ~2018 (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | tax
- From 2023, Belarusian organisations and ИП must self-assess VAT when buying services from foreign companies that aren't registered in Belarus. | https://ilex.by/osnovnye-nalogovye-izmeneniya-po-rabotam-uslugam-s-2023-g/ | iLex | 2023 (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | tax
- Russia: the standard VAT rate is 22% from 1 Jan 2026 (425-ФЗ of 28.11.2025), with no transition period. | https://ppt.ru/news/idea/byudzhetnyy-paket-na-2026-2028-gody-i-nalogovye-izmeneniya ; https://buh.ru/articles/izmeneniya-zakonodatelstva-s-1-yanvarya-2026-goda-polnyy-obzor-bez-syurprizov.html | ppt.ru; buh.ru | 2025–2026 (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | med-high | tax
- Russia УСН: 228-ФЗ of 04.07.2026 reportedly keeps the VAT exemption for УСН taxpayers with revenue up to 20M ₽ for 2026–2028. Sources disagree on the step-downs after that (15M and 10M in 2029/2030 or later). Reduced УСН VAT rates of 5% and 7% are available. | https://spmag.ru/news/usn-porog-vyruchki-dlya-osvobozhdeniya-ot-nds-mogut-sohranit ; https://konsol.pro/blog/nds-pri-usn | spmag; Konsol | 2026-07 (snippet) | accessed 2026-10-11 | med | tax
