- C1a (Russia App Store payments stopped 1 Apr 2026): verified | "no longer available in Russia unless you have funds in your Apple Account balance" | https://support.apple.com/en-us/126891 | Apple Support | 2026-05-29 (page date; effective 2026-04-01) | accessed 2026-10-11 | high
  - Independent corroboration: MacTrast, "Apple Turns Off App Store and Services Payments in Russia", https://www.mactrast.com/2026/04/apple-turns-off-app-store-and-services-payments-in-russia/ (2026-04). Apple also says payment processing can no longer add funds to the balance, and carrier billing is gone. Lapsed subscriptions are cancelled.
- C1b (Belarus storefront payment status 2026): unverified (no primary source) / secondary only | "цены выставлены в долларах США" | https://www.iphones.ru/iNotes/kazahstan-protiv-belorussii-kuda-perenesti-akkaunt-apple-posle-rossii-v-2026-godu-i-pochemu-odin-variant-luchshe | iPhones.ru | updated 2026-07-15 (first published around April 2026) | accessed 2026-10-11 | low-medium
  - Secondary sources say the BY storefront still takes cards, but only from banks that are not under sanctions. Prices are in USD, carrier billing is not available in Belarus, and gift cards are practically unobtainable. The source names no specific card network (Belkart, Visa or Mastercard). I found no Apple doc that restricts Belarus. Treat IAP as working but with friction. Many Belarusian bank cards (from sanctioned banks) will fail.
- C2a (Guideline 3.2.1(vii) monetary gift): verified | "100% of the funds go to the receiver of the gift" | https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ | Apple Developer | current version, date not captured | accessed 2026-10-11 | high
  - Important caveat in the same clause: "a gift that is connected to or associated at any point in time with receiving digital content or services must use in-app purchase."
- C2b (3.1.3(b) Multiplatform Services): verified (wording) | "provided those items are also available as in-app purchases within the app" | same URL | Apple Developer | accessed 2026-10-11 | high
  - So 3.1.3(b) does NOT allow a web-only subscription that has no IAP equivalent.
- C2c (Apple guidance on Russia web-only subscriptions): unverified | no Russia mention found in the guidelines text read (first 100k chars), and no developer news item found | — | — | — | accessed 2026-10-11 | low
- C3 (3% Russian ad levy, affiliate/CPA applicability): partially verified / disputed on affiliate | "рекламный сбор платит только тот, кто заключил договор с рекламодателем" (1C ITS summary of Постановление № 1224) | https://its.1c.ru/db/content/newscomm/src/494159.htm | 1C ITS | 2025 | accessed 2026-10-11 | medium
  - Confirmed: 3% of income from distributing internet advertising aimed at consumers in the RF, effective 2025-04-01. Payers are распространители (including site owners and bloggers), operators of ad systems, and intermediaries. RKN calculates the amount from ERIR data, and it is paid quarterly. Note: the article number differs across sources (18.1 or 18.2 of 38-ФЗ). I could not read garant.ru (encoding error).
  - Affiliate/CPA: I found no RKN or FAS explanation. Secondary sources (vc.ru, nalog-nalog.ru) contradict each other. The common reading is that whoever receives money for distributing the ad pays, and under the chain rule that is the first party contracted with the advertiser (for example the CPA network or Yandex Distribution). A webmaster further down the chain pays only if there is an agency chain. Status: unresolved, needs a lawyer or an official RKN request.
- C4 (Belarus: foreign e-service providers register and pay 20% VAT): verified | "Ставка налога при оказании услуг в электронной форме составляет 20%" | https://nalog.gov.by/electronic_vat/ and https://pravo.by/novosti/obshchestvenno-politicheskie-i-v-oblasti-prava/2026/june/93711/ | МНС РБ / pravo.by | 2026-06 | accessed 2026-10-11 | medium-high (quotes come from search-result extracts, because both .by hosts refused a direct fetch: ECONNREFUSED)
  - The registering inspectorate is "инспекция МНС по г. Минску" (neg.by, https://neg.by/novosti/otkrytj/postanovka-na-uchet-inostrannoj-organizacii-po-jelektronnoj-torgovle-uslugi-v-jelektronnoj-forme/). Registration is free and online via "е-НДС иностранного лица". Returns are quarterly, due by the 20th. A sale counts as Belarusian based on IP, bank, phone code or location. The authorities can suspend the site for non-compliance. 110 foreign companies were registered as of 2026-06-01. EAEU regime since 2024-04-01: a Russian seller to Belarusian individuals pays VAT in Belarus and must register there. New application forms apply from 2026-03-11.

- C6 (ЮKassa: only Mir/NSPK-partner cards; can a Belarusian card pay?): verified with nuance | "Из зарубежных карт принимаются: ... БЕЛКАРТ (Беларусь) ... Мир" | https://yookassa.ru/docs/support/payments/accept-methods | ЮKassa | no date (© 2026) | accessed 2026-10-11 | high
  - By default ЮKassa accepts cards issued in Russia, across all networks. The foreign cards it accepts are АПРА, БЕЛКАРТ, Корти Милли, Express Pay and Мир. Belarusian Visa/Mastercard cards are not listed, so they are effectively not accepted. Belkart and Belkart-Mir cards can pay. The list "может меняться".
